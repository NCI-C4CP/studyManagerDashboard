Engaging men in research

Previous studies have suggested that men may be less likely to join long-term research studies than women. Some have investigated possible reasons for this gap. To explore this topic and learn how to get more people, especially men, involved in research, three Connect health care systems conducted one-on-one interviews and focus groups. They asked: What makes someone want to join a study like Connect? What holds them back? How can researchers build trust? What they learned will help us reach more people and encourage more men to take part in research studies like Connect.
